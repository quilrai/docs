# Display and profile

Two small settings pages: **Display** for presentation preferences, and **User Profile** for your own account details.

## Display

<ConsolePath console="QuilrAI Console" path={['Settings', 'Display']} />

### Workspace-wide

These apply to everyone in your organization.

| Setting | Options |
|---|---|
| **Timezone** | The timezone console timestamps use. Select **Save** to apply. |
| **Theme** | **Light** or **Dark** |

### This browser only

These apply only to the browser you are using.

| Setting | What it does |
|---|---|
| Quilr Assist bubble | Show or hide the floating Quilr Assist bubble |
| Page guide | Show or hide the page-guide help button |
| Bubble size | **Default** or **Compact** |
| Command palette history | Keep or clear your recent command palette (Cmd+K) searches |

## User Profile

<ConsolePath console="QuilrAI Console" path={['Settings', 'User Profile']} />

Your **Name** and **Email**, synced from your identity provider. They are read-only here; change them in your identity provider.
